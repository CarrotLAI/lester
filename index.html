About

I'm a Front-End Developer with 2+ years of professional experience in developing and maintaining web-based business applications using C#, .NET, Blazor WebAssembly, and MudBlazor. My experience revolves around building practical software solutions that support real business operations, including inventory management, leasing, accounting, and internal management systems.

Beyond writing code, I take an active role in understanding business requirements, planning application workflows, designing user interfaces, and translating complex processes into maintainable and user-friendly applications.

I've also taken on frontend leadership responsibilities, guiding fellow developers, coordinating frontend implementation, reviewing application flows, troubleshooting technical issues, and continuously improving existing systems.

I believe that good software development goes beyond making an application work. It means creating solutions that are reliable, maintainable, efficient, and genuinely useful to the people who use them. I'm continuously expanding my skills, exploring modern frontend technologies, and learning new approaches to building better software.
